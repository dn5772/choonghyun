The Lord’s NZ Chuhyun Church is:

- Essentially the Body of Christ (Ephesians 1:23), sent into the world as an apostolic (missional and incarnational John 20:21) presence to bring salvation to the world (John 3:16; Ephesians 1:10).

- Our purpose is to glorify God’s name, embody and expand His Kingdom, and see His will be done on earth as it is in heaven.

- As an apostolic community, our mission is twofold: vertically, to worship (love) the Triune God, and horizontally, to engage in mission (love) toward the world.

We warmly welcome you in the love of Jesus.
