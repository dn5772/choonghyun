1. Mission Statement (Why do we exist?)
– We are a missional and incarnational Spirit-filled community, growing to reflect the divinity and humanity of Jesus Christ.

2. Vision (What do we aim to become?)
– To serve the reawakening of the Pyongyang Great Revival and the peaceful reunification of the Korean Peninsula.

3. Core Values (How do we carry out our work?)
– Spirituality, Love, and the Manifestation of the Holy Spirit.
