Welcome Note

The Lord’s NZ Chuhyun Church is:

  • Essentially the Body of Christ (Ephesians 1:23), sent into the world as an apostolic (missional and incarnational John 20:21) presence to bring salvation to the world (John 3:16; Ephesians 1:10).

  • Our purpose is to glorify God’s name, embody and expand His Kingdom, and see His will be done on earth as it is in heaven.

  • As an apostolic community, our mission is twofold: vertically, to worship (love) the Triune God, and horizontally, to engage in mission (love) toward the world.

We warmly welcome you in the love of Jesus.

Pastor

I was baptised in 1995 and have since been engaged in pastoral ministry and teaching.

In 2007, I earned an M.Div. from the Hapdong Theological Seminary in Korea, followed by a Th.M. and, in 2020, a Ph.D. in Practical Theology from Chongshin University.

I have served in Suwon Jeil Church, focusing on ministry to Chinese residents in Korea, pastoral work, and mission efforts related to Korean reunification.

Currently, I serve as the director of “Bridge,” a non-profit organisation, and am a member of the NEAR Team of International Interserve, working toward reconciliation, cooperation, and peaceful reunification of the Korean Peninsula.

Church Vision

  1. Mission Statement (Why do we exist?) – We are a missional and incarnational Spirit-filled community, growing to reflect the divinity and humanity of Jesus Christ.

  2. Vision (What do we aim to become?) – To serve the reawakening of the Pyongyang Great Revival and the peaceful reunification of the Korean Peninsula.

  3. Core Values (How do we carry out our work?) – Spirituality, Love, and the Manifestation of the Holy Spirit.
